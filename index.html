Our Story

Built by Hand,
Built to Last

At Texas Workshop, we believe furniture should outlive trends. Our master craftspeople have spent decades perfecting joinery, carving, and finishing techniques passed down through generations.

Every piece – from a stately wardrobe to an intimate side table – begins as a sketch and ends as a heirloom. We source only sustainably harvested timber and finish each surface by hand to bring out the natural warmth of the wood.

– The Texas Workshop Team
